About the ESG Certification
Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Certification

The Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Certification is a structured and independent certification scheme designed to assess, verify, and recognize organizations that demonstrate responsible, ethical, and sustainable business practices in alignment with internationally recognized ESG principles, standards, and regulatory expectations.

The certification provides a comprehensive framework for evaluating how organizations manage environmental impacts, fulfill social responsibilities, and maintain effective governance systems. It confirms that organizations operate in a transparent, accountable, and responsible manner while continuously improving their performance in sustainability, compliance, and risk management.

The ESG Certification focuses on three core pillars that define responsible organizational performance:

Environmental Responsibility
This pillar evaluates how organizations manage their environmental footprint and resources, including energy efficiency, waste management, environmental protection, regulatory compliance, and continuous improvement in environmental performance.

Social Responsibility
This pillar assesses how organizations support employee well-being, maintain safe and fair working environments, protect customer interests, and contribute positively to communities through responsible and ethical practices.

Corporate Governance
This pillar examines leadership structures, risk management systems, regulatory compliance, internal controls, and ethical business conduct to ensure transparency, accountability, and sound decision-making.

Obtaining the ESG Certification demonstrates that an organization has implemented structured policies, systems, and controls that support responsible operations and sustainable growth. It enhances organizational credibility, strengthens stakeholder confidence, and supports alignment with national priorities and international sustainability standards.

The ESG Certification represents an ongoing commitment to responsible business practices and continuous improvement. Certified organizations are required to maintain compliance with certification requirements and undergo periodic assessments to ensure sustained performance and long-term resilience.
