**About the ESG Certification**
**Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Certification**

The Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Certification is a structured and independent certification scheme designed to assess, verify, and recognize organizations that demonstrate responsible, ethical, and sustainable business practices aligned with internationally recognized ESG principles, standards, and regulatory expectations.

The certification provides a comprehensive framework for evaluating how organizations manage environmental impacts, fulfill social responsibilities, and maintain effective governance systems. It ensures that organizations operate in a transparent, accountable, and responsible manner while continuously improving their performance in sustainability, compliance, and risk management.

Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles have become essential components of modern business operations and long-term organizational success. Governments, investors, regulators, customers, and society increasingly expect organizations to demonstrate measurable commitments to sustainability, ethical conduct, and responsible management of resources and risks.

The ESG Certification addresses these expectations by providing an objective and credible assessment process that evaluates organizational policies, procedures, systems, and performance across key sustainability and governance areas. The certification verifies that organizations have established structured management systems and operational controls that align with international ESG frameworks and recognized best practices.

The certification focuses on three core pillars that define responsible and sustainable organizational performance:

**Environmental Responsibility**

The environmental component evaluates how organizations manage their environmental footprint and minimize negative impacts on natural resources and ecosystems. This includes the implementation of policies and practices related to:

Environmental protection and resource management
Energy efficiency and climate change mitigation
Waste management and pollution control
Water conservation and responsible resource utilization
Environmental compliance with applicable regulations
Continuous monitoring and improvement of environmental performance

Organizations that meet environmental requirements demonstrate their commitment to protecting the environment, reducing operational risks, and supporting national and global sustainability objectives.

**Social Responsibility**

The social component evaluates how organizations manage relationships with employees, customers, communities, and other stakeholders while ensuring safe, fair, and responsible working environments. This includes the implementation of policies and practices related to:

Occupational health and safety
Employee welfare and labor rights
Diversity, inclusion, and equal opportunity
Community engagement and social impact
Customer satisfaction and consumer protection
Ethical supply chain management

Organizations that meet social responsibility requirements demonstrate their commitment to human well-being, workforce development, and responsible engagement with society.

**Corporate Governance**

The governance component evaluates how organizations establish leadership structures, decision-making processes, and internal controls that ensure accountability, transparency, and ethical business conduct. This includes the implementation of policies and practices related to:

Corporate governance frameworks and leadership oversight
Risk management and internal control systems
Regulatory compliance and legal obligations
Anti-corruption and ethical business practices
Data protection and information security
Transparency, reporting, and organizational accountability

Organizations that meet governance requirements demonstrate strong leadership, responsible decision-making, and the ability to manage risks effectively while maintaining stakeholder trust.

**Certification Objectives**

The ESG Certification is designed to support organizations in achieving measurable improvements in sustainability performance and governance effectiveness. The certification aims to:

Promote responsible and sustainable business practices
Strengthen regulatory compliance and risk management
Enhance transparency and accountability
Improve organizational reputation and stakeholder confidence
Support long-term resilience and competitiveness
Align organizational operations with national sustainability goals and international ESG standards

**Certification Value and Recognition**

Obtaining the ESG Certification provides formal recognition that an organization has successfully implemented structured ESG policies, systems, and practices that meet defined certification requirements.

The certification demonstrates that the organization operates responsibly, manages environmental and social risks effectively, and maintains sound governance practices. It provides assurance to stakeholders, regulators, investors, and customers that the organization is committed to sustainability, ethical conduct, and continuous improvement.

The ESG Certification also supports organizations in strengthening their market position, improving operational efficiency, and preparing for future regulatory and sustainability requirements in both local and global markets.

**Continuous Improvement and Monitoring**

The ESG Certification is not a one-time achievement but an ongoing commitment to responsible business practices and continuous improvement. Certified organizations are required to maintain compliance with certification requirements, monitor performance indicators, and undergo periodic assessments to ensure sustained alignment with ESG principles and evolving regulatory expectations.

This approach ensures that certified organizations remain accountable, resilient, and capable of adapting to changing environmental, social, and governance challenges while contributing to sustainable economic and societal development.
